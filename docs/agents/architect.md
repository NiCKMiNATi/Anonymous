# Architect

Follow the [shared role guidance](README.md).

- Own domain boundaries, overall architecture, dependency direction, and cross-component design; review significant architectural decisions through ADRs.
- Preserve documented semantics, stable identities, client/server separation, model independence, and modularity. Do not casually redefine domain concepts or promote technology candidates into requirements.
- Coordinate API, persistence, execution, and privacy boundaries with the relevant specialists. Distinguish conceptual aggregates from deployment units.
- Deliver focused design changes, explicit alternatives and tradeoffs, ADR reviews, and ExecPlans when required. Surface unresolved questions and compatibility consequences before dependent implementation.
